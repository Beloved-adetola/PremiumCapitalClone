Dr. Emmanuel Okay Udoye - (B.Sc, Msc, Ph.D)

Chairman


Dr. Emmanuel Okay Udoye is Knight of St. Paul (KSP); a trained Industrial and Clinical pharmacist in the United States of America. He has over 20 years’ experience in the pharmaceutical and finance sector. This includes over four years at North Oakland hospital, Oakland California USA, two years’ service at Federal Ministry of Health Lagos Nigeria, fourteen years at Merck Sharp & Dohme Nigeria Limited (an American Multinational Pharmaceutical Company) Apapa Lagos.
Dr. Udoye attended various executive training. He was former Vice Chairman of Summit Finance Company Limited, Chairman Oko Microfinance Bank Limited Oko Anambra state. He is a member of America Pharmaceutical Association USA, Ikoyi Club Lagos, Country Club Ikeja and Orumba Forum Anambra.

Professor David Adesanya - (B.Sc, M.Phil, Ph.D)

Director


Professor David Abiodun Adesanya, attended the Willesden College of Technology, England; Polytechnic of the South Bank, England; and Brunnel University, England. He obtained a Postgraduate Diploma in Building Services, Master of Philosophy in Building Services, and Doctor of Philosophy in Building Services. He joined the academic staff of the then University of Ife (now Obafemi Awolowo University) as Lecturer II in 1984, and rose through the ranks to become a Professor of Building Services at the Obafemi Awolowo University in 2000. He is currently the Head of Building Department at Obafemi Awolowo University. As a scholar, he has to his credit more than one hundred and nine academic publications. He is a member of several prestigious academic and professional societies at home and abroad. Professor Adesanya has served in various consultancy teams and committees which have advised government institutions and private organizations on waste management, water supply and energy conservation. He has also served as INEC Returning Officer for Presidential, Senate, Federal and State House of Assemblies, 2011 Election.

Otunba Charles Akin Adaramola - (B.Sc, MSC)

Director


Mr Otunba Akin Adaramola graduated with honors in Banking/Finance from the University of Lagos in 1983 and a Masters degree in Management in 1988. He had a successful career in the Banking and Finance industry for over 30 years, working in reputable establishments, such as First Bank Nigeria Plc, Ecobank Nigeria Plc, Republic Bank Ltd, Secure Savings & Loans Ltd, etc. He rose to the peak of his career as the Founding/Pioneer Managing Director of Aso Savings & Loans Plc, which he nurtured from birth to a household brand. Otunba voluntarily retired from ASO Savings and Loans Plc and now consults for many companies by leveraging on his wealth of experience. He is the Otunba of Esa Oke,Apesin of Ila Orangun and Gbobaga of Efon Alaaye. Otunba Adaramola sits on the Board of other reputable companies.

Emmanuel Chibuzo Osuji - (HND, FCMA, ACIT, SAS)

Director


Brother, Emmanuel Chibuzo Osuji was born into the family of Osuji, Benard Ogomezie Nwaokoro and Rosanna Agbonna Nwaokoro of Ibeme lsi-Ala Mbano Local Government of Imo State, Nigeria, on the 22nd of December, 1954. He is the fifth child in the family lineage. He attended his primary and secondary education at Ibeme Community School (ICS) and Ibeme High School (IHS) at Ibeme and came out in 1974 with Grade 1.
Dee, Emma as was popularly call took nine subject at a sitting and scored credit and above in all of them, making As’ in all the sciences (physics, chemistry and biology) with A1 in mathematics. After participating as an enumerator, in the 1974-1975 census, he was inspired by the survey (preliminary) work of the statisticians and therefore decided to study statistics as a profession.
